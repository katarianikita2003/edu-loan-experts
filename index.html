Frequently Asked Questions

What is an education loan?

An education loan is a loan that can help students and families meet eligible higher-education expenses. The terms, eligibility and approval depend on the lender.

Can I get an education loan for studying abroad?

Education loans may be available for eligible study-abroad programs. Eligibility, eligible expenses and loan terms depend on the lender and the student's application.

What documents are generally required?

Documents can include academic records, admission or offer documents, identity and address proof, financial documents and other information requested by the lender.

How is education loan EMI calculated?

EMI generally depends on the loan amount, applicable interest rate and repayment tenure. You can use our EMI calculator to estimate the monthly repayment.

Does Edu Loan Experts guarantee loan approval?

No. Loan approval is determined by the concerned lender based on its eligibility criteria, documentation, assessment and applicable policies.
